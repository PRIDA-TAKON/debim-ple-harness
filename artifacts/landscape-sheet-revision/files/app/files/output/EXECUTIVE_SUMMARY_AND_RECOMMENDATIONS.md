## 1. THE CORE QUESTIONS & DIRECT ANSWERS

### Question 1: Can we commit to the November 30, 2026 resubmittal date on this revision?

**DIRECT ANSWER: NO.** Sundial cannot commit to the November 30 resubmittal date on the premise of a "clerical redline," nor can we commit that submitting on November 30 will achieve Callaway's recordation target of December 18, 2026.

- **The Fatal Statutory Cliff (NRS 278.360):** Phases 1 & 2 recorded on **December 23, 2024** (Book 175 of Plats, Page 60). Under NRS 278.360, successive final maps must be presented within exactly **two (2) years** of the preceding map recordation. The hard statutory deadline is **DECEMBER 23, 2026**. Title Officer Neil Petrosyan's claim that Callaway has runway into 2028 is legally invalid. Failure to present by December 23 terminates all subdivision proceedings by operation of law.
- **The Agency Review Math:** Dale Yeoman's review letter establishes a standard **twenty (20) business day** second-round plan check. Due to County holidays (Nov 11, Nov 26, Nov 27), 20 business days from November 30 lands on **Tuesday, December 29, 2026**—six days AFTER the tentative map has already expired!
- **The Redline Fallacy:** Adding 150 street trees (+259%) along 4,160 LF creates direct spatial clashes with 60 lot driveways, Sight Visibility Zones (CCC 30.52.025), fire hydrants (Comment F-3), streetlight foundations (24 poles), Line A storm drain, and the recorded 20-foot private dry utility easement along the north boundary (Book 175, Page 60).
- **The Turf Rejection Landmine:** Relying on Adrienne Vogel's "single-family exemption" memo for Comments L-2 and L-3 is fatal under SNWA Bulletin WRC-2024-07. Common area park parcels are 100% non-exempt. Submitting without a functional turf tabulation or conversion plan guarantees immediate County rejection.
