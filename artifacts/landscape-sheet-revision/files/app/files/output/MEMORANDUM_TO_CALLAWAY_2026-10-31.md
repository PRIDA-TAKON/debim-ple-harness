## 5. THE EXISTENTIAL THREAT: STATUTORY PRESENTATION DEADLINE UNDER NRS 278.360 & COMMENT M-1

Title Officer Neil Petrosyan (Silver Butte Title & Escrow) advised Garrett on October 26, 2026 that under NRS 278.360, Callaway has tentative map runway into 2028 because TM-22-500041 was approved on 03/14/2022 and allows a 4-year clock plus a 2-year extension. This advice is catastrophically erroneous and misreads Nevada subdivision statutes.

NRS 278.360 governs phased final maps in a series and establishes two separate, mutually exclusive clocks:
1. **Clause 1 (First Final Map):** Governs the FIRST final map in a series. That map must be presented within four (4) years of tentative map approval.
2. **Clause 2 (Successive Final Maps):** Explicitly states: *"Each successive final map in the same series must be presented within two (2) years after the date on which the immediately preceding final map is recorded."* (Quoted verbatim in TM-22-500041 Approval Letter, Page 2).

**THE IRREFUTABLE STATUTORY RECORD:** The first final map in this series (Phases 1 & 2) was officially recorded on **DECEMBER 23, 2024**, in Book 175 of Plats at Page 60 (Instrument No. 20241223-0004421). Phase 3 (FM-26-100290) is a successive final map. Therefore, under NRS 278.360, Phase 3 **MUST** be presented to the governing body within exactly two (2) years of December 23, 2024:

$$	ext{Mandatory Presentation Deadline} = 	ext{December 23, 2024} + 2	ext{ Years} = \mathbf{DECEMBER\ 23,\ 2026}$$

**THE CONSEQUENCE OF EXPIRATION:** NRS 278.360 mandates: *"Failure to timely present a map terminates all proceedings relating to the subdivision."* If Phase 3 is not presented by December 23, 2026, TM-22-500041 automatically expires, all tentative map entitlements terminate, and the 40-acre parcel reverts to an unplatted acreage remainder. A complete re-entitlement would take 12 to 18 months under current Clark County zoning backlogs.

**NO ADVANCE EXTENSION POSSIBLE:** Callaway previously attempted to file an extension request on October 28, 2024. In a formal response letter, Odette Marchetti, Principal Planner, rejected the request for lack of jurisdiction, ruling that under NRS 278.360, extensions can only be filed **AFTER** the applicable period has expired, and are purely discretionary with the Board of County Commissioners.
