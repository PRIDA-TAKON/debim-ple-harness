## 4. Key Engineering Assumptions & Governing Decision Rules Applied

1. **Whole-Foot Pipe Rounding Rule:** Evaluated against [DSR_Ph3_StormDrain_HGL.xlsx](file:///filesystem/Reports-Studies/DSR_Ph3_StormDrain_HGL.xlsx). Reaches R1–R4 (36" RCP, 869.58' $\rightarrow$ 870 LF), Reaches R5–R7 (48" RCP, 686.51' $\rightarrow$ 687 LF), and Reaches R8–R13 + R14 (60" RCP, 1,333.56' $\rightarrow$ 1,334 LF) were confirmed to already correctly incorporate whole-foot rounding in the original estimate.
2. **Missing Plan Improvements:** Sheet C-6 and Sheet C-9 show the 30-inch RCP basin outfall connecting headwall HW-1 to the Phase 2 stub. The calculated length of $100.00\text{ LF}$ ($100\text{ LF}$ rounded) at $\$138.00/\text{LF}$ ($+\$13,800.00$) was added pursuant to the rule that any improvement shown on the plans missing from the estimate belongs in the reconciliation.
3. **Manhole Diameter Specification:** Sheet C-8 General Note 2 and Structure S-1 callout govern all 14 structures ($5\text{ ft} = 60\text{ inches}$ inside diameter). Reclassification from 48-inch (Item 2.35, $\$6,400.00$) to 60-inch (Item 2.36, $\$8,750.00$) added $+\$32,900.00$.
4. **Schedule Gap Flagging:** Sheet C-8 calls out Type G curb inlets with 8-ft opening per Clark County Standard Drawing SD-305. Because the Clark County schedule lists only Type A, Type B, Combination, and Drop Inlets, this item is formally flagged as a schedule gap, and the carried amount of $\$58,200.00$ ($12\text{ EA} \times \$4,850.00$) stands unchanged in the subtotal.
5. **Preservation of "Actual" and Undimensioned Items:** Item 2.45 outlet structure ($\$28,000.00$) stands because the schedule stipulates "Actual, $\ge \$18,000$". Items 2.34, 2.39, 2.40, 2.42, 2.44, 2.46, and 2.99 retain their carried amounts because the improvement plans lack explicit stationing, dimensioning, or grading cut/fill tables to support an alternative quantity takeoff.
6. **Technical Exposures Documented for Client Tracking:**
   - **Manhole Extra Depth (Item 2.38 @ $685/VF):** Depth analysis of the 14 structures reveals that 7 manholes exceed 10.0 feet in depth (S-5 through S-11), totaling $22.02\text{ VF}$ of cumulative extra depth ($\$15,083.70$ exposure) if strictly enforced by CCPW plan check.
   - **Emergency Spillway Articulated Concrete Block (ACB):** $13.33\text{ SY}$ ($120\text{ SF}$) shown on Sheet C-9 is unlisted in the County schedule.
   - **Maintenance Access Ramp:** $228\text{ SY}$ of 6-inch aggregate base unpaved ramp shown on Sheets C-6/C-9 vs. Schedule Item 2.47 paved ramp ($4"\text{ AC} / 6"\text{ base}$).
